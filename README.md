# Adidas-Dashboard
## 1) Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

**Project Title:** Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of Adidas sales performance across different regions, states, products, and retailers. It helps stakeholders monitor sales trends, profitability, product performance, and regional sales distribution for data-driven decision-making.

---

## 2) Purpose

The main purpose of this dashboard is to:

* Analyze overall Adidas sales performance.
* Track monthly sales trends and seasonality.
* Identify top-performing products and retailers.
* Compare sales across regions and states.
* Monitor operating profit and profit margins.
* Support business decisions through interactive data visualization.

### Business Questions Answered:

* What is the total sales revenue generated?
* Which month achieved the highest sales?
* Which product category contributes the most revenue?
* Which retailer generates the highest sales?
* Which states perform best in sales?
* What is the average operating margin?

---

## 3) Tech Stack

### Microsoft Excel

* Data cleaning and preprocessing.
* Handling missing values and duplicates.
* Formatting and validating raw sales data.

### SQL

* Data extraction and transformation.
* Aggregations and KPI calculations.
* Data preparation for reporting.

### Power BI

* Data modeling and relationships.
* DAX measures and KPIs.
* Interactive dashboard creation.
* Visualizations and business insights.

---

## 4) Data Source

**Source:** [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The dataset contains Adidas sales transactions including:

* Invoice Date
* Region
* State
* Product Category
* Retailer
* Units Sold
* Price per Unit
* Total Sales
* Operating Profit
* Operating Margin

---

## 5) Features

### KPI Cards

* Total Sales: **$163M**
* Operating Profit: **$60.56M**
* Units Sold: **407K**
* Price per Unit: **$50**
* Average Operating Margin: **42%**

### Monthly Sales Trend Analysis

* Line chart showing sales performance month-by-month.
* Helps identify peak and low sales periods.
* Highest sales observed in **September ($21.2M)**.

### Geographic Sales Analysis

* Interactive map visualization.
* Displays sales distribution across states.
* Enables regional performance comparison.

### Regional Sales Breakdown

* Donut chart for sales contribution by region.
* Quick view of regional revenue share.

### Product Performance Analysis

* Product-wise sales comparison.
* Identifies best-selling Adidas product categories.
* Supports inventory and marketing decisions.

### Retailer Performance Analysis

* Sales comparison among retailers.
* Top retailers:

  * Foot Locker
  * Sports Direct
  * Walmart
  * West Gear
  * Amazon

### Interactive Filters

* Region Slicer
* Invoice Date Range Filter
* Dynamic cross-filtering across all visuals

### Business Insights

* Peak sales occur during Q3 (July–September).
* Foot Locker contributes the highest retailer sales.
* Men's Street Footwear is the top-selling product category.
* Strong operating margin (42%) indicates healthy profitability.
* Sales performance varies significantly across regions and states.

**Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI, SQL, Excel**

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard using Adidas sales data from Kaggle to analyze revenue, profitability, product performance, retailer contribution, and regional sales trends. Performed data cleaning in Excel, data transformation using SQL, and created KPI-driven visualizations with DAX measures in Power BI.
